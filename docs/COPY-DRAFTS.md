## 3. About page (route /atelier, page handle `atelier`)

**Title:** About SISU

SISU is a small homeware studio in Amsterdam, making cushions from deadstock fabric.

Deadstock is the surplus of the textile industry — ends of rolls, over-orders, fabrics from
discontinued collections. It's often beautiful, always finite, and usually headed for waste.
SISU buys it from EU-based suppliers and turns it into considered, well-made cushions:
feather-filled, concealed zips, sharp corners, finished by hand.

SISU is run by Jessie, a British expat in Amsterdam. She spent more than ten years on the
operational side of fashion — building a European operations office from scratch, managing
logistics and wholesale for international brands — before starting SISU as the hands-on,
small-batch counterpoint to a career spent in operations at scale.

Because every fabric run is limited, every design is too. When a fabric is used up, that
cushion won't be remade. We think that's a feature.

*(Optional closing line — confirm with Jessie:)* ⚠️ Sisu is a Finnish word for a particular
kind of quiet determination. It felt right.
